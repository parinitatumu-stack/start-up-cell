student-innovation-start-up-cell

Where ideas become ventures.

Startup Cell is a full-stack web platform designed to help educational institutions manage the entire startup incubation journey—from idea submission to Demo Day.

Instead of relying on scattered Google Forms, emails, and spreadsheets, the platform provides one centralized space where students can register startups, submit proposals, connect with mentors, track milestones, and evaluate their pitch readiness using AI.

✨ Key Features

🚀 Startup Registration

Submit and manage startup ideas with detailed business information.

📄 Proposal Management

Upload, review, and track startup proposals throughout the incubation process.

👨‍🏫 Mentor Matching

Assign mentors based on startup domain and expertise.

📈 Milestone Tracking

Monitor startup progress throughout the incubation journey.

🤖 AI Pitch Readiness Engine

One of the core features of the platform.

Using Google's Gemini API, startups are evaluated across:

Business Clarity
Problem Definition
Innovation
Market Potential
Solution Strength
Team Readiness
Business Model Viability

The platform generates:

Overall Readiness Score
Strengths
Weaknesses
Risk Analysis
Actionable Suggestions

🏛️ Role-Based Dashboards

Separate experiences for Students and Administrators.

🔐 Secure Authentication

Google OAuth authentication powered by Supabase.

🔔 Notifications

Stay updated on mentor assignments, proposal approvals, milestones, and Demo Day announcements.
